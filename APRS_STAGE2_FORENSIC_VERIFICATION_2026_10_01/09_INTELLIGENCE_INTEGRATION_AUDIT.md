## 1. Investigation of Analytical Downstream Consumers

The core value proposition of APRS is that observed research signals feed into automated decision intelligence:
- Evidence Graph
- Product Intelligence Subtabs
- Opportunity Scorecard
- Customer Pain Map
- Competitor Gaps & Trends
- Kill Switch Verification

---

## 2. Code Trace of Intelligence Entrypoints

### Entrypoint 1: `core/intelligence_engine.py`
- Method: `build_evidence_graph(product_id: str)`
- Code Trace:
  ```python
  cursor.execute("SELECT signal_type, conclusion, confidence, source FROM intelligence_evidence WHERE product_id = ?", (product_id,))
  ```
- **Finding:** Reads exclusively from the SQLite `intelligence_evidence` table. Because `core/research_sources/` does not write to this table, the Evidence Graph receives **zero data** from any of the 11 new source adapters.

### Entrypoint 2: `Customer Pain Map` & `Competitor Gaps`
- Located in `web/app.py:tab_intelligence`
- Code Trace:
  - Pain points are rendered from `product_intelligence.pain_map_json` (generated by `tools/defect_miner.py` and `models/nim_cluster.py`).
  - Gaps are rendered from `product_intelligence.gaps_json`.
- **Finding:** Neither Reddit nor Scavio adapter findings reach these JSON columns.

### Entrypoint 3: `Kill Switch Verification`
- Method: `core/intelligence_engine.py:verify_kill_switch_triggers()`
- Code Trace: Checks margin thresholds, regulatory flags, and defect ratios from pre-existing dossier fields.
- **Finding:** No signals from the new research sources influence kill switch execution.

---

## 3. Classification

**ADAPTER-LEVEL IMPLEMENTATION ONLY**

The research source subsystem stops at:
`adapter → normalized evidence → registry (in-memory)`

It does **NOT** reach:
- Evidence Graph
- Product Intelligence
- Opportunity Scorecards
- Decision Ledger

The new research adapters are completely disconnected from all downstream intelligence and scoring systems.
